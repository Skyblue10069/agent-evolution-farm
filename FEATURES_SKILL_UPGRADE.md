# Agent Evolution — Skill & Multitasking Upgrade

## New adaptive skill layer
Agents retain the original rule: every agent starts with zero developed skills and no pre-assigned profession. The new skill engine adds 30 meta-skills that grow from observed outcomes:

- learning_speed
- memory_management
- reasoning
- planning
- research
- problem_solving
- creativity
- decision_making
- adaptability
- self_criticism
- prediction
- negotiation
- communication
- delegation
- leadership
- teamwork
- resource_management
- risk_management
- opportunity_discovery
- business_strategy
- customer_understanding
- quality_control
- automation_design
- technical_skill
- economic_intelligence
- competitive_intelligence
- portfolio_management
- failure_recovery
- meta_learning
- self_improvement

## Evolving traits
Agents also develop behavioral traits such as curiosity, focus, patience, risk tolerance, creativity, discipline, cooperation, and independence. Traits influence learning and planning rather than assigning fixed jobs.

## Meta-learning
Each agent records recent outcome signals, skill gaps, learning history, and lessons. The system uses those observations to adjust future capability and identify weak areas worth improving.

## Multitasking
Agents can work on multiple legitimate tasks concurrently. Their task capacity is learned from planning, focus, and discipline and is bounded at 6 concurrent tasks per agent for stability. Tasks remain independently tracked and still require the existing artifact, QA, persistence, and proof gates before completion.

Multitasking does not create money. Verified revenue still comes only from provider-confirmed payment records.

## Dynamic teams
Agents with sufficient leadership/delegation ability can form internal teams from their own sub-agent hierarchy. Team records are planning structures only; they do not fabricate customers, contracts, or revenue.

## Recursive hierarchy
Sub-agents inherit evolutionary traits/lineage, start with zero money and zero developed skills, and may independently decide to create their own sub-agents. The hierarchy remains voluntary and bounded by the farm resource cap.

## Self-improvement
The existing bounded self-code system remains active. Agents may improve their private strategy modules through syntax validation and sandbox-style restrictions, while core payment, security, accounting, and orchestration paths remain protected.
